# Dough mockups

Read `dough-brand` and `dough-profile-layout` first.

**Principles:** a mockup shows the client's work in context and never competes with it. Neutral, unified backgrounds (cream, warm grey, or navy); no black boxes behind products. Cut out products cleanly (alpha edges, no halos) or place on a tinted ground that matches the page.

**Recipes**
- *Menu screens:* portrait 9:16 digital boards, black bezel `#0B0B0B` 12–14px, hung on a tiled wall with a coloured baseboard, or three screens in perspective (±30° rotateY) with floor reflection and a soft brand-colour glow.
- *Mobile menu (QR):* phones 9:16 with the screen filling edge to edge, radius 44px outer / 32px screen, no notch overlapping content, slight rotation (±6°) for the side phones; keep the menu's own background colour consistent with its print version.
- *Packaging:* straight-on cutouts in a row on a tinted ground, shared baseline and soft shadow.
- *Booths & installations:* full-bleed render left, short caption right; state if built or concept.
- *3D / illuminated standee:* stack 10–14 offset layers for depth, tilt rotateY −24° / rotateX 3°, brand-colour backlight, glowing floor pool, faint mirrored reflection. Use high-res cutouts and vector logos (upscaled logos look soft).
- *Image quality:* 2× the displayed size; compress to JPEG/WebP; never crop hands, faces or product tops.
