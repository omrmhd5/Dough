# Dough social & campaign

Read `dough-brand` first.

**Approach (from Lux, Knorr, Kufta, Akleh, Primos work):** take a mainstream or traditional brand and give it a Gen-Z voice through social direction: a recognisable always-on feed system plus one hero stunt per quarter.

**Feed system:** 1080 × 1350 (feed), 1080 × 1920 (story/reel). One consistent template family: big lowercase headline, one product or character, brand colours of the CLIENT with Dough staying invisible. Alternate hero, product, quote and behind-the-scenes frames.

**Copy:** short, spoken, bilingual-ready (Egyptian Arabic in Alexandria, English in Montserrat). Humour over hype; never more than one idea per post. Avoid emoji unless the client brand uses them.

**Campaign pattern:** insight, one mechanic people can join (e.g. Kufta Ramadan chips-bag giveaway, Knorr April Fools), a rollout across social + OOH, and a measured result. Present with brief / what we did / result.

**Deliverables checklist:** content pillars, grid system, 12-post sample, 3 story templates, caption tone guide, results page.
