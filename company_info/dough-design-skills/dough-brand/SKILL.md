# Dough brand

**Who:** Dough, a creative consultancy ("where ideas take shapes."). Values: innovation, flexibility, creativity, holistic. Voice: energetic, youthful, inspiring. Warm kitchen metaphor (knead, rise, bake) used lightly, never as food clip-art.

## Colour (monochromatic blue; no other brand colours)
- Light `#FFFCF7` (RGB 255/252/247, CMYK 0/0/2/0)
- Sparkle `#C4D9EB` (196/217/235, 21/7/2/0)
- Water `#457D9E` (69/125/158, 76/43/24/2)
- Dark `#122940` (18/41/64, 95/78/48/51)
Allowed logo pairings only: dark on light/sparkle, light on dark/water. Client work may add client colours; Dough chrome stays blue.

## Type
- Latin: **Montserrat** only (Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700, ExtraBold 800; Light 300 for large editorial text).
- Arabic: **Alexandria** (Light to ExtraBold), matched weights to Montserrat.
- Wordmark: Galak Round (logo artwork only; never retype it).
- Lowercase is the house style for headlines. Display lines pair a light weight with one bold key word, ending in a full stop.
- Specimen: Headline ExtraBold 70/70pt; sub-head Bold 30/33; sub-head Medium 25/28; body Regular 12/14; caption 9/10.5.

## Logo
- Clear space = height of the "u". Min size 30 × 7.7 mm print, 85 × 22 px web.
- Never: stretch, drop shadow, outline, rotate, change font, use caps.
- Placement: square formats left/right aligned; rectangles centred or left/right.

## Graphic language
The **dough shape** is the key visual: soft, circular/oval, changeable form, never sharp corners. Can become a character with arms and legs (playful, quirky). Use sparingly as an accent, not wallpaper.

## Content rules
Keep user copy verbatim. No emoji. No filler or stats for decoration. Generous negative space. Minimum text contrast 4.5:1.
